## What You Get — Free, Forever

The following features are **included at no cost.** This is not a preview, and it's not a trial. These features remain available to Inkrepublik Tattoo Studio for as long as the site is maintained.

---

### 1. A Complete Public Website

A modern, mobile-friendly website that reflects the quality of your studio:

- **Home page** — a full hero section, artist highlights, services overview, and customer testimonials
- **Artists page** — profiles for each artist with bios, specialties, and portfolio galleries
- **Services page** — your full catalogue with categories (tattoos, piercings, jewellery, consultations) and pricing
- **Gallery** — an aggregated view of every artist's portfolio work
- **Reviews page** — real client testimonials, pulled from your Google and Facebook reviews
- **Contact page** — a working enquiry form that lands directly in your inbox

The site is designed to feel like **your studio** — dark, ornate, high-contrast, with the same visual language as your existing logo.

---

### 2. An Online Booking System

Clients can request appointments directly through the site, **24 hours a day, from any device.**

Here's how it works, from your perspective:

1. **A client visits your site** and clicks "Book Now"
2. **They fill in a simple form** — their name, contact details, preferred date and time, and a description of what they'd like
3. **You receive an email notification** with all the details
4. **You confirm, reschedule, or decline** through your admin panel
5. **The client receives a confirmation email** with a private link to view, reschedule, or cancel their booking — **no account needed**

**Why this matters:** Most tattoo studios lose bookings because they only accept DMs or phone calls. This system gives you a structured, trackable way to manage every request — with a full audit trail of every interaction.

---

### 3. An Admin Panel — Your Studio Control Centre

Behind the scenes, you get a private dashboard where you control everything:

- **Bookings dashboard** — all requests in one place with search, filters, and status tracking
- **Weekly calendar view** — see every booking at a glance, with colour-coding by artist
- **Booking actions** — confirm, reschedule, decline, or mark as complete with one click
- **Artist management** — add artists, upload profile photos and portfolio pieces
- **Services manager** — update prices, add new services, deactivate old ones
- **Review moderation** — approve, feature, or edit testimonials before they go live
- **Contact inbox** — all enquiry form submissions in one place
- **Site settings** — edit your address, hours, socials, and copy without touching code

**You don't need technical skills to run this.** If you can use Facebook, you can use the admin panel.

---

### 4. Search Engine Foundations

So people can actually find you:

- **Google-friendly page structure** — proper titles, meta descriptions, semantic HTML
- **Mobile-first design** — Google prioritises mobile-friendly sites in search
- **Structured data** — tells Google exactly what your business is (a tattoo studio in Hout Bay)
- **Google Business Profile linkup** — I'll help you connect your existing profile (which already has 123 five-star reviews) to the new site

**Realistic expectations:** Your studio will start appearing in local searches for "tattoo studio Hout Bay" within weeks. Broader SEO (ranking for "best tattoo studio Cape Town") takes months, not days. Anyone who promises you otherwise is lying.

### What's the Catch?

I want to be completely transparent about two limitations of the free tier:

**1. Data can reset.** The site is hosted on a free cloud tier. If the server is restarted by the hosting provider — which happens every few weeks — any bookings or uploaded photos that accumulated since the last reset will be lost. The site itself never goes down, and your seeded content (services, artists, reviews) is always there. But live client data isn't permanent.

**2. No custom domain.** The site runs on `inkrepublik.onrender.com`, not `inkrepublikcpt.com`. If you want your own domain, that's part of the paid upgrade (see Page 4).

**Neither of these is a dealbreaker for trying it.** You can run the site for a month, see if clients use the booking system, and decide whether the upgrades are worth it. If they're not — you've lost nothing.
